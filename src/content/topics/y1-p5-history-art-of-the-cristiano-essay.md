An essay should be written by doing all the readings then closing the books and simply writing for 1 hour.

## Cambridge Essay Structure

An essay in persuasion - that means take a **strong** position - back up your claims with evidence and discuss how the shortcoming of your argument are irrelevant.

## Supo 1

- INTRO - Define ambiguous terms (e.g. define macro and micro invention)
  - *else* have no introduction
- PARAGRAPHS - Focus on the question and pick out some key points/arguments
  - Then have a strong evidence base (this consists of key names, dates technologies etc.)
  - E.g./ POINT: Adoption of an invention is a slow process of macro and micro invention.
  - EXPLAIN: The theory is that micro invention is a process by doing.
  - EVIDENCE: For instance the steam engine was invented (Newcomen) in 1712. But only adopted a century later. (Can go beyond reading list for these examples)
- CONC - Do not repeat yourself, deliver 'final blow' not rehash old arguments.

Essay writing tips (handwritten 1 hour):

- Don't use contractions
- Focus on the question (e.g. if says growth and productivity do not mention the word welfare.)
- Have **strong evidence** base
  - Remember key dates, examples and numbers to support arguments (theory) with concrete examples.
  - Can go beyond reading list for this
  - E.g. show effect of how innovation improves growth by taking the use case of the mechanical loom.

## Supo 2

- QUALTIATIVE JUMP from 2:1 -> 1st. Taking a defensive approach, pre-emptively countering the counter arguments to your argument.
- STRENGTHENING argument by showing it holds even under multiple definitions of the 'Transatlantic slave economy'.

NOTE: It is of critical importance to read the most latest readings into a topic. They argue on top of the old arguments. For instance, the latest data shows that crowding out did indeed exist if we look at quantities not interest rates (because usury laws prevent interest rate going up and so crowding out is observed in terms of quantities not rates).

Likewise, latest readings show that slave economy should be considered from the broader perspective of than just plantations. But how plantations affected prosperity of regions across the world (NOTE: It is best to argue anglo/american are one and same...)
